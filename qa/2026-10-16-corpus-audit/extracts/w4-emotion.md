## Top 10 candidate principles for coordinator coverage check

These are the ten most directly applicable candidates for character performance and audience emotion. Their skill coverage is **unverified** in this lane; this list does not assert that no existing skill covers them.

1. **Picard:** New appraisal of agency, intent, or goal impact can change emotion and action even while the physical trigger persists.
2. **Scherer et al.:** Emotional quality depends on subjective appraisals of relevance, consequences, coping, and social standards; reappraisal can update it.
3. **Combei and Reggi:** Viewers interpret emotional stance from combined verbal and visual choices in context.
4. **Meiselman:** Face, voice, body, and context jointly shape emotion recognition; conflicting channels can change the inferred emotion.
5. **Lazarus:** Viewer framing and identification or distancing can change responses to the same film scene.
6. **Gross:** Regulation choices can intervene at the situation, attention, appraisal, or response stage and should adapt as circumstances change.
7. **Handbook of Emotions:** Audience knowledge and point of view can separate empathy with a protagonist from sympathy for them.
8. **Handbook of Affective Sciences:** Familiar appraisal patterns support recognition; selective challenge can invite curiosity and exploration.
9. **Oxford Handbook of Affective Computing:** Expression appropriateness depends on social role, cultural norms, and interaction context; mixed emotions can be performed together.
10. **Hogan:** Viewers use the character’s eliciting situation together with expressive/action responses to infer emotion.
