## How these sources constrain the method

- **Mechanism/coherence:** connect the proposed mark, palette roles, and type relationship to the supplied brief (K-brand). A single named central mechanism is this workflow's compact review contract for that relationship, not a requirement that all successful brands have only one association.
- **Mark routing:** retain lettermark, pictorial, abstract, mascot, and emblem capabilities. Use the route cues as optional comparisons, not necessary/sufficient conditions for a category. Require a brief-specific reason and an actual application check for the selected route. No source establishes the old “only for” exclusions.
- **Pairing/anatomy:** inspect actual stroke weight, width, curvature, and proportions in the rendered specimen; explain a similar or complementary relationship (A-logo-font). Contrast can complement the mark: literal anatomical matching is not mandatory. Required language coverage is checked in actual fonts, not inferred from style.
- **Hierarchy:** one dominant expressive mechanism with quieter support is the default constrained direction format, adapted from A-logo's “Own one thing” and A-pair's headline/body hierarchy. It is not a universal aesthetic law. An explicit user multi-device direction remains supported: record its intended reading order and inspect competing weights instead of rejecting it solely for having several devices.
- **Optical balance:** inspect whether icon or type unintentionally overwhelms the other at intended sizes (A-logo-font). Record the visible imbalance and the proposed scale/spacing correction. Equal numeric boxes are not proof of optical balance, nor does a visual judgment prove exact geometry or audience recognition.

The fixed candidate counts, maximum families, symbol-only phase, preview/state payloads, and approval/dependency semantics remain local output/implementation contracts. Their exact counts are not attributed to these sources. No remote generation or paid-success behavior is established by these readings.
