# Enneagram lens: nine motive lenses as creative questions

Read when `typology_mode` is `enneagram` or `both`. The desires/fears below are short original paraphrases of the Enneagram Institute's type pages [14–22] — a creative lens for what a character tries to secure and avoid, not a typology verdict, test content, or a fixed reaction table.

## Nine motive lenses (paraphrased, not quoted)

| Type | What they seek to secure | What they work to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Being good, right, balanced — integrity intact | Being defective, corrupt, wrong |
| 2 | Being loved, needed | Being unworthy of love |
| 3 | Being valued, worthwhile, seen as accomplished | Being worthless, exposed as a failure |
| 4 | Identity and personal significance — being truly oneself | Having no identity or meaning |
| 5 | Competence, capability, mastery | Being helpless, incapable, invaded |
| 6 | Security, support, reliable guidance | Being without support or orientation |
| 7 | Satisfaction, fulfillment, freedom | Deprivation, pain, being trapped |
| 8 | Self-protection, control of their own life | Being harmed, controlled, vulnerable |
| 9 | Inner stability, peace, harmony | Loss, separation, conflict that fragments |

## Interpretation rules

For the active core type, write: `supplied | proposal | undecided`, the character's personal version of the desire/avoidance (not the row's generic wording), the **recurring securing strategy** (how they reliably get/protect it in relationships), and **pressure vs. settled behavior** — what they do when the fear is live versus when it quiets. Use the design questions: What does this character do, visibly, when the feared state approaches? What does securing look like in this specific relationship?

- **Wings** (adjacent-type influence [12]): optional — propose one only when the user asks or it explains a concrete variation of this character. Never obligatory.
- **Stress/growth directions** [12]: theoretical reference for writing, not a scene-state calculator. Do not add growth arcs, redemption, or collapse that the synopsis lacks just because a direction table suggests them.
- The lens explores motive; it does not fix reactions. Two type-6 characters can secure safety differently — one through rules, one through alliances.

## Misuse boundaries

Same as the MBTI lens: no typing real people or undelegated existing characters; supplied types recorded as supplied, not verified; no type→appearance/voice mapping; gaps between supplied type and approved behavior are named, behavior wins.

## Evidence status

The nine-type system, wings, and direction theory come from the Enneagram Institute's own description — a tradition's model statement, not independent validation [12,14–22]. A 2021 systematic review (Hook et al., 104 samples) found mixed reliability/validity evidence, factor analyses mostly recovering fewer than nine factors, and little research support for wings or type movement [3]. Use the lenses as motive questions; claim no empirical grounding. See `sources.md`.
