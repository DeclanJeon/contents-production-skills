## V1 — ORBIT, 14 seconds

**Vehicle SSOT / reveal.** Approved *verbal* identity: wedge body, one amber side stripe, circular lamps, four five-spoke wheels. Preserve wheel count and spoke count, stripe side and continuity, silhouette, and lamp geometry in all views and reflections. `ORBIT` may appear as plain proposed end-copy; logo artwork, placement, and any badge are **unapproved**, so do not fabricate or certify a logo. No range, safety, speed, or handling claim.

| Final window | Shot, motion phase, and camera relation | Edit/reveal |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4.2 s | `SH01` Ordinary rolling on shallow wet slate at constant **body** speed. Side view holds a readable wheel and contact patch; camera may track, but its movement is specified separately from vehicle translation. | Reveal wedge, amber stripe, circular lamp; preserve wheel/ground relation through cut. |
| 4.2–6.0 s | `SH02` Approach luminous water; wheels remain slate-supported until visible lift begins. | Cut out during approach, cut in before support is lost so the transition is legible. |
| 6.0–10.3 s | `SH03` Deliberate fantasy levitation over luminous water. Body rises; wheel–ground contact and ordinary rolling constraint cease. Shadow/reflection move with body height; water response follows the passage. | Hold the fantasy state long enough to read it as intentional. |
| 10.3–12.2 s | `SH04` Descend onto wet slate; suspension/contact settles before the vehicle stops. | Landing and stop are separate visible phases. |
| 12.2–14 s | `SH05` Stopped vehicle reveal; clean silhouette, stripe, lamps, and four-wheel count. | Optional plain “ORBIT” title after motion settles; no invented badge. |

**Motion/state handoff.** In `SH01–SH02` and after landing in `SH04`, ordinary no-slip rolling is the intended physical rule: wheel angular progression must agree with forward ground travel and tire radius, allowing for perspective and motion blur. Camera tracking can make body movement small on screen while the ground and contact patches still move relative to it. During unsupported levitation in `SH03`, **no-slip is N/A**; wheel rotation may continue as a designed fantasy motion, but it must not be judged against ground travel. At landing, restore coherent support, contact shadow, reflection, and wheel response before the stop.

The director’s **single still with apparently backward spokes does not alone fail motion QA**. Sample successive frames across rolling, lift, flight, landing, and stop; compare spoke phase, hub translation, ground texture, contact patch, camera motion, shadow and reflection. Apparent reversal can result from temporal sampling or shutter effects; persistent incompatible rotation/contact across inspectable frames is a candidate motion failure. Record the exact observed interval before assigning severity.

Wet slate may leave spray or sheen on tires/body. Keep any visible water residue through flight and return until a shown reset or plausible dissipation; do not make it vanish at a cut. A wake belongs only where vehicle movement actually disturbs water; match its direction and decay to the passage. **Sand residue: N/A** because no sand is specified.

Model identity, caption timing/content, sound, reuse rights, and reference-derived claims remain unverified.
