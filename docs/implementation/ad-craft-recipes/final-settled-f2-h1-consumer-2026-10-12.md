## H1 — Lantern Quay, 16 seconds

**Status:** text-only design proposal. `SH` and `C` IDs below are local handoff labels, not registered project IDs. The inn, route, host, and garden are fictional. Times are proposed final timeline seconds, start inclusive and end exclusive. No media was inspected.

The ordinary-space anchor is the **south vestibule entrance → east turn at the brass lantern → curtain threshold → tea room → north garden window**. The return follows the curtain and lantern back toward the south entrance. The miniature garden appears only within the window wipe; the next guest POV restores the ordinary north window and the established direction of travel. The wipe is a fantasy view, not a change to the inn’s floorplan.
