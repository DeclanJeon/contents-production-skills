# Photo Guide

What to upload for Soul training.

All images must depict the same consenting person. Verify upload and reusable-model
training authority before transmitting photos. The 5–20 count is a documented provider
constraint; the preferences below are authored selection heuristics, not measured guarantees
or additional provider validation requirements.

Direct provider read 2026-10-17: `xd://mcp__higgsfield_show_characters` images/medias
and ambiguity guard; CLI 1.1.26 `soul-id create --help` image count. They support 5–20,
one identity and transport constraints, not a measured photo-selection optimum.
The 1024px target, JPEG/PNG preferences and variety suggestions below are selection
heuristics, not an exhaustive accepted-format contract; inspect upload constraints.

## Quantity

- Minimum 5, maximum 20.
- 8–12 is an authored starting range, not a validated optimum or required target.

## Content

- Clear face, eyes visible.
- Single person per photo.
- No heavy filters, no sunglasses.

## Variety

Authored heuristic: select useful angle/lighting variety within clear, identity-consistent
photos of the same person. More variation is not inherently better: occlusion, blur,
filters, and inconsistent appearance can reduce usefulness. No quality improvement is guaranteed.

- Multiple angles: front, 3/4 left, 3/4 right, slight up/down.
- Different lighting: indoor, outdoor, soft, harsh.
- Different expressions: neutral, smiling, talking.
- Different distances: head shot, head-and-shoulders, full body.

## Quality

- Sharp, in-focus.
- Resolution ≥ 1024×1024 ideal.
- JPEG or PNG.

## Avoid

- Group photos.
- Heavy makeup not normally worn.
- Costumes / cosplay.
- Hats covering the face.
- Same pose repeated.
