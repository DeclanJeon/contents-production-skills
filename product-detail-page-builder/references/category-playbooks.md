# Category Playbooks

Select modules from verified facts and real product images. This is a question checklist, not a fixed page template. Omit sections with no evidence.

## Shared buyer questions

1. What is the product and what is included?
2. What verified use or benefit does it provide?
3. Which details, dimensions, or compatibility facts affect the purchase?
4. How is it used, fitted, installed, stored, or cared for?
5. Which decision-relevant uncertainty should be stated rather than guessed?

## Apparel and accessories

Use actual product angles, visible seams/hardware, user-provided measurements, size charts, confirmed colors/variants, and supplied care details. Never infer fiber composition, stretch, warmth, waterproofing, fit, or size from a single photo.

## Home and kitchen

Prefer visible construction, supplied dimensions/capacity, documented operation, and actual use photos. Do not invent load, heat, food-contact safety, capacity, mounting strength, or before/after outcomes.

## Beauty and personal care

Use supplied packaging, verified ingredients, visible texture, and documented instructions. Do not invent efficacy, medical effect, safety, hypoallergenic status, certification, or suitable audiences.

## Electronics and accessories

Use visible controls/ports and supplied specifications, compatibility, charging, and operating instructions. Do not infer hidden connectors, protocol support, battery performance, water resistance, or certifications.

## Food and supplements

Use actual packaging and supplied label data. Ingredients, allergens, origin, nutrition, health claims, storage, and shelf life require explicit evidence. Leave claims out when the source is missing.

## Module selection

Common module types are `hero`, `benefit`, `feature`, `detail`, `scene`, `use`, `specs`, `package`, `care`, and `closing`. The builder permits 1–12 modules but should choose only those that answer a buyer question without repeating another module.
