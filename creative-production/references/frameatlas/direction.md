# FrameAtlas — direction by stage

Source: FrameAtlas, original public-use field guides marked CC0-1.0. Read the relevant source at the stage below; these questions supplement the current owner's contract and never change scope or authority.

- **Brief and story:** [Foundations](foundations.md). Record the intended audience feeling as one sentence. Carry the applicable material, light, spatial or behavior constraints and a detail that would break them in the existing spine/LOOK. Separate proposed direction from fixed constraints.
- **Scene and technical board:** [Scene Frameworks](scenes.md). For the current beat/shot, identify visible action or relationship, composition/blocking, and what new information or emotional turn the camera reveals. A static shot is appropriate when movement has no purpose. Pass the evidence cue with existing beat/cut/panel IDs.
- **Sequence and visual QA:** [Field Method](method.md). Read the actual shot order: action/reaction, screen direction, eye line, light, identity/prop state and each cut's distinct contribution. Record discrepancies against actual panel/clip IDs, repair only affected parts, then reinspect the sequence.

The three rules, three frames and four beats in the source are exercises, not quotas or mandatory formats.
